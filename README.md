# Hi, I'm Alexandra Delverdier

**Analytics engineer specialised in healthcare data · Former nurse**

I spent eight years as a nurse, in neurology and psychiatry wards and then in my own private practice. I moved into data after working in medtech, where I saw how much clinical teams struggle to get clear answers from their data. Today I build reliable data pipelines and AI tools for healthcare, with the clinical judgment I gained at the bedside.
